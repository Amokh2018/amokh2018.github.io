THE APPROACH

Make the path
understandable.

Four stages, adapted to the problem. Open each stage to see what it needs to establish.

01Frame

Define the decision.

Clarify the users, workflow, available data, constraints and evidence needed to judge success.

02Design

Choose a testable architecture.

Compare approaches, document trade-offs and define evaluation before expanding the system.

03Validate

Test the critical assumptions.

Build a focused prototype with representative examples. Review the evidence and make limitations visible.

04Transfer

Prepare the next stage.

Document ownership, deployment needs and operating practices, with knowledge transfer to the team.
